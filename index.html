    Before you sign

    Is ZenAi Center live?

    The mall runs today as a working prototype you can walk through. Checkout, payouts and the food delivery partner connect at launch. Early-access brands get first pick of units.

    Do I need 3D or design skills?

    No. Pick materials, moods and fixtures from menus, upload product photos and hit Publish. We set it up with you on the walkthrough.

    Who ships the products?

    You do. Orders come to you with the customer’s details, and you fulfill them with your own inventory and carrier.

    How do tokens work?

    Shoppers earn tokens only by playing in the arcade. Tokens are never sold and have no cash value. You decide which basic items, if any, can be redeemed with tokens.

    What if I want to cancel?

    Rent is month to month. When you cancel, your store comes down and the unit shows as available again.
